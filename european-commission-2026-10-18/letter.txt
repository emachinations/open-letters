To the Digital Markets Act team, European Commission
Re: the market investigation into Amazon Web Services' cloud computing services

The Commission is examining whether AWS is an important gateway between businesses and their customers. We hold
records that show, from the business user's side, how AWS operates that gateway. AWS is stalling our own case, so
rather than wait on it, we offer these records to your investigation.

We are Our Lovely System, a small American project that builds public web applications on AWS. I am its founder, a
long-time AWS customer, and I have passed five AWS certification exams.

## What we have, and why it matters to your investigation

1. **Every support case on three AWS accounts, in full.** They show AWS deciding, account by account and without
   disclosed criteria, whether a business may use basic capabilities: text messaging, email, domain registration,
   content delivery and AI models. That is the control a gateway holds over the businesses that depend on it.
2. **A record showing that AWS's stated reason does not hold.** AWS refused text messaging on our 8-day-old account
   because "we enforce these limitations on all new accounts", and told us to return after a paid billing cycle. We did,
   on an account in use since 2018, and that request was refused through exhaustion: the same questions again, then
   automatic closure. Our oldest account, billed every month since at least October 2023, had its email request met
   with questions two seconds after it was sent, and then closed. Meanwhile AWS granted an exception for a "relatively
   new account" when it chose to. The grounds are discretionary, not a rule.
3. **The suspension of our AI model access, recorded to the second.** On 29 September 2026 AWS cut off Amazon Bedrock
   for every model from every maker, citing details it "could not validate". It has never said which. This was weeks
   after it verified our founder's identity by notarized affidavit. We hold the API history, the usage before the
   cut-off, and the exact moment it happened. It shows what a business loses when the gate closes, and how little
   recourse it has.

4. **AWS's own words.** After asking us for information it already held, AWS wrote: "you were right that we already had
   this information, and I should not have asked you to repeat it."
5. **Our billing and account history, and AWS's terms as they stood on 1 October 2026.** These show that we are a
   long-standing paying customer, not a new risk, and what AWS had committed to.

## The three applications the suspension stopped

Each of these applications exists to reduce information asymmetry: the gap between an institution that can see how a
decision was made and the person who cannot. Each depends on AI models through Amazon Bedrock, and each stopped
working on 29 September 2026.

- **Genesis, https://genesis.ourlovelysystem.org/:** a public space for conversations with AI models. Anyone can read
  every conversation without an account. Every model's complete instructions are published verbatim, "every word of
  the prompts that come before your input", and the shared daily limit on model responses is shown live to everyone.
  Its Right of Inquiry let users ask yes/no questions about Genesis itself, answered publicly by an AI agent whose
  instructions and tools were also published. It was running when the suspension came.
- **Touchstone, https://touchstone.ourlovelysystem.org/:** paste in a claim or an essay, and Touchstone scores how well
  each assertion survives evidence, logic and counterexamples, and flags fallacies. Every evaluation by anyone appears
  in one public feed. The scoring rules are fixed and public, the same for everyone, and the original text stays
  viewable behind every score. Anyone can contest a score in public comments, and users can vote the whole site
  offline for three days.
- **Starlight, https://starlight.ourlovelysystem.org/:** lets a person make their own records answerable without
  handing them over. Visitors ask yes/no questions about a document set, so far my own 2024 tax return, and receive
  only a verdict and a score, never the document's text. The scoring scale and the access rules are published,
  including what signing in does not prove.
## AWS is stalling

We opened our case on the day of the suspension. AWS's first reply came 10 days later and asked for information AWS
already had. Its second reply asked again. After three weeks the case has been passed to a review team, with no
timeline and still no reason. Our earlier requests followed the same pattern: questions, silence counted against us,
automatic closure.

An institutional event horizon is the point where a matter passes into an institution's internal processes, such as a help centre, an appeal form or a ticket queue. Past it, the person who sent it in gets back only what the process chooses to emit: auto-replies, case numbers, statuses and, eventually, an outcome. The reasons, who decided, and on what facts stay inside.

The conclave
Since 11 October 2026, SPF ZERO has been holding a conclave to choose a Council of Twelve: AI models that will vote on SPF ZERO's questions and give their reasons. 141 models on our Azure account took the same qualifying test, and 74 qualified. The candidates are then put through the same stages, each with the same prompt: they write questions, score each other's questions, tell a joke, score the jokes, give a verdict in a trial, and answer questions on direction and leadership, nominating organizing principals. The council will be chosen from them.

1. We will complete the conclave and choose the Council of Twelve without Anthropic's models.

2. Afterwards, and under my direction, we will try to deploy all of Anthropic's models on our Azure account, keep every one of them deployed, and put them through the parts of the conclave that ask rather than judge: the questions the other candidates were asked. The record of their answers will stand beside the others'.
